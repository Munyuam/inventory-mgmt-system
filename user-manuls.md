# User Operations Manual

### 1. Introduction

The Inventory Management System is a desktop app designed to help you track products, manage suppliers, and record purchases and sales. It provides a real-time view of your stock levels and financial performance through an easy-to-use interface.

### 2. Getting Started

* **Login** : Open the app and log in with your username and password. The default admin account is

  username **admin** and password is **admin**

  admin. For security, please change the admin password immediately after your first login.
* **Dashboard Overview** : The dashboard gives you an instant summary of your business:
* **Key Stats** : Total products in stock, items running low, total inventory value, and estimated profit.
* **Stock by Category** : A pie chart showing how your inventory is divided among different categories.
* **Top Items** : A list of the products that are moved or issued most frequently.

### 3. Managing Inventory

* **Product List** : Go to the "Products" page to see all your items. You can search for products by name, category, or supplier.
* **Adding Stock (Procurement)** :

1. **Supplier** : Select an existing supplier or enter a new one.
2. **Invoice Info** : Enter the invoice number, date, and how you will pay (e.g., Cash or Credit).
3. **Add Items** : List each product you bought, including its cost, selling price, and quantity.
4. **Save** : The system will automatically update your stock levels and record the transaction.

* **Issuing Items** : When items are sold or moved out, use the "Issue" feature to record the change.
* **Stock Adjustments** : If you find that the actual stock on your shelf doesn't match the system (e.g., due to damage), use the "Adjustment" tool to correct the numbers.

### 4. Reports and Summaries

* **Weekly Reports** : The "Summary" page shows your weekly activity, including how much was bought, issued, and the revenue generated.
* **Export to Excel** : You can save any weekly report as an Excel file for your records or further analysis.
* **Activity History** : Select any product to see its full history—every time it was bought, sold, or adjusted.

### 5. Settings and Profile

* **User Accounts** : Admins can add new users, change roles (Admin or Staff), or remove accounts.
* **Your Profile** : You can update your username, email, and password.
* **Appearance** : Switch between Light and Dark modes to suit your preference.
